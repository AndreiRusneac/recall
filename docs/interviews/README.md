# Customer interviews

One file per conversation, named `YYYY-MM-DD-business-type.md`. Do not record personal details
of patients or anything the person asked to keep private.

## Questions to ask

1. How do patients book today, and how do they reach you (phone, WhatsApp, which number)?
2. What do you use to keep the schedule (software, paper diary, Google Calendar)?
3. How many appointments do you have in a typical week?
4. How many people did not show up last week? What does one missed appointment cost you?
5. Do you remind patients today? Who does it, how, and how long does it take?
6. Do you offer online booking? If not, why not?
7. If reminders cut no-shows in half, what would that be worth to you per month?
